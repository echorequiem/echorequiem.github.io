About

Turns MusicXML scores into a scrolling sheet-music video in which the sounding note glows in the colour you choose. Add a background image or video, a music track and intro / outro screens; the result is exported frame by frame as MP4 or WebM, up to 4K. No internet connection needed: everything stays on your computer.

Features

  • Opens MusicXML (.musicxml, .xml, .mxl) and reads tempo and tempo changes from the file
  • Each sounding note glows for its own length; trills shimmer, slurs flow like water
  • MP4 (H.264 + AAC) or WebM (VP9 + Opus) · 720p to 4K · 24–120 fps · 16:9, 9:16, 1:1, 4:3, 4:5
  • Background image or video, haze and ember effects, a music track with millisecond offset
  • Intro and outro screens (video and / or three text templates); the score settles in and wipes away
  • Preview of the finished video before rendering, saved projects, HD score-strip images, 10 interface languages
